**Short description**
```

**Full description**
```
Battle Hymn is a free rhythm bullet-heaven: part music game, part survival
arena. A wizard stands at the bottom of the screen while enemies close in from
every direction. Above him a musical staff scrolls by — every incoming note is
an enemy.

Read the highlighted note, press the right key on the piano keyboard, and the
wizard fires a spell to destroy it. What matters is the NOTE, not the octave:
if it's an A, play any A and you're good. Hit it on the beat to earn a gem —
collect five and gain an extra life.

FEATURES
• Unique rhythm + bullet-heaven mash-up
• Realistic piano keyboard with white and black keys
• Two-octave staff with treble clef and ledger lines
• Note labels your way: solfège (Do Re Mi), letters (C D E) or none
• Adjustable tempo (30–200 BPM) and three difficulty levels
• Enemy variety: fast, armored and mini-bosses
• Roguelite progression: earn crystals, buy permanent upgrades
• End-of-run grade (S/A/B…) and saved high score
• All audio synthesized in real time — no copyrighted assets

100% FREE, NO ADS. If you enjoy it, there's an optional "Buy me a coffee" tip
to support development — it unlocks nothing, it's just a thank-you.

Grab your wand, find the rhythm, and turn music into magic.
```
